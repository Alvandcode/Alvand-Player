Alvand Player — your world, your music.

A minimal, beautiful Android music player that adapts to your songs:

🎨 DYNAMIC COLORS — the background, progress bar and controls take their
color from the current song cover, with smooth animated transitions.

🌙 SLEEP TIMER — fall asleep to music: 5–90 min presets or end of song,
with gentle fade-out and a live countdown chip.

🏠 HOME-SCREEN WIDGET — play, pause and skip without opening the app.

🎧 EVERY FORMAT — mp3, aac, ogg, opus, flac, wav, m4a, amr, midi + HLS/DASH
streams and direct links.

🎚 PRO SOUND — 5-band equalizer with presets, BassBoost, volume boost up to
+10 dB and hiss/hum denoise for clearer vocals.

📝 LYRICS — embedded tags, sidecar .lrc files, free online search (LRCLIB)
and manual paste, synced line by line.

🌍 17 LANGUAGES — switch inside the app, no restart needed.

🔔 FULL INTEGRATION — notification, lock-screen and Bluetooth/media-output
controls, background playback that survives closing the app.

Free, fast and private — your music stays on your device.
